Working method

A proof should survive
more than one kind of scrutiny.

01

Resolve the structure

Reduce a spectral question to the exact scalar, geometric, or variational mechanism that controls it.

02

Stress-test the claim

Pair proofs with numerical experiments, edge cases, and explicit audit documents that expose the fragile steps.

03

Formalize the spine

Translate the theorem’s dependency graph into Lean, with pinned environments and transparent axiom reports.
