01 / Operator theory

Crouzeix’s conjecture: the 2-spectral-set inequality

The manuscript develops a proof of the 2-spectral-set inequality for the numerical range of every complex matrix. The repository also contains a pinned Lean 4 formalization of the matrix-polynomial statement and an axiom audit.

02 / Pseudospectral analysis

Connectedness threshold for the open Hatano–Nelson chain

For the tridiagonal matrix family defined below, the manuscript gives a necessary-and-sufficient criterion for connectedness of the Euclidean pseudospectrum. It also proves strict decrease of the barrier, sharp-order bounds, and a fixed-parameter asymptotic for the critical site count.

Definitions used in the stated results
  • Criterion
  • Barrier
  • Sharp-order bound

Formalization scope. The Lean 4 project covers the labelled mathematical statements in the audited manuscript. Physical interpretation and floating-point figures are outside that formalization scope.

About

Research interests

I am a neurosurgeon interested in math, working at the Department of Neurosurgery of Peking Union Medical College Hospital.

My current projects concern operator theory, numerical linear algebra, matrix pseudospectra, and non-Hermitian spectral problems. The repositories on this page provide manuscript sources, computational code where applicable, and Lean 4 formalization materials with documented scope.
